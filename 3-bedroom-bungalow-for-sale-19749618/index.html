Description

18 Malham Close is an excellently-presented, three-bedroom bungalow providing modern accommodation. The stylish, front-facing Lounge is open to dining space and a well-appointed kitchen; accommodation continuing with three double bedrooms, a versatile office/study, bathroom and conservatory at the rear. Occupying a sizeable plot with driveway parking for multiple vehicles at the front, lawned child and pet friendly garden at the rear, the property will suit a range of potential purchasers.

The property is located to an attractive position with easy access to the city of Lincoln, and its full range of services, amenities, social and leisure attractions and rail links to London and the East Coast mainline. The property is located to the Western side of the city, with the A46 ring-road easily accessible.
